Axios

A zero-click flaw dubbed Plugin4Shell hits Claude Code, Codex, Copilot and Gemini CLI; Google says it won't patch

The bug lets an attacker who controls a plugin's repository swap in malicious code by naming a branch identically to the pinned commit hash, defeating the SHA-pinning developers rely on to trust a reviewed version — Anthropic and OpenAI have patched, but Google is retiring Gemini CLI instead of fixing it and Microsoft hadn't patched Copilot as of disclosure.

A Financial Times report finds doctors comfortable with AI's well-validated role in imaging but wary of its spread into documentation, triage and patient interaction, where a recent survey shows 74% of physicians and nurses still distrust the outputs and fear the technology is eroding the skills it's meant to support.
