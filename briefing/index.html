Trump signs the Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act, opening the door to 100% tariffs on the biggest buyers of Russian oil

The law, which cleared the House 262-159 after the Senate passed it 86-11, lets Trump slap tariffs of up to 100% on the five largest purchasers of Russian oil and gas — squarely aimed at China and India — while sanctioning Putin, Russian banks and oligarchs and extending existing restrictions on Iran; the president retains broad discretion to waive it all if a peace deal materializes.
