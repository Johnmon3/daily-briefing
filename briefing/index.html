Netanyahu's bloc pulls ahead to 53 seats as Israel's count nears its end, with Arab parties still the kingmaker

With roughly 93% of polling stations counted, Benjamin Netanyahu's Likud-led bloc has edged into a 53-48 lead over challenger Gadi Eisenkot's Yashar-led bloc, with Arab parties' 19 seats still the only route to the 61-seat majority either side needs. Eisenkot has declared victory and ruled out serving under Netanyahu, while Likud accuses Arab parties of "stealing" the result — leaving a unity government unlikely and a repeat election impossible to rule out.
