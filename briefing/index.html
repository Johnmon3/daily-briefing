Ethiopia and Eritrea each tell the UN Security Council the other started a war of aggression, as drone strikes hit near Adigrat

Ethiopia's letter to the Council invoked its Article 51 right to self-defense after it said up to 8,000 Eritrean troops pushed as far as 60km into its territory; Eritrea's reply called the move a "declaration of war" and asked the UN for deterrent measures. ACLED now reports Ethiopian drone strikes on Eritrean forces near Adigrat, even as Eritrean troops pull back from the town — a war that keeps escalating exactly as both capitals insist they're only defending themselves.
