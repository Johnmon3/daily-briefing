Top story

Anthropic files for a Nasdaq IPO that could be one of the largest ever

The S-1 discloses an $80.7 billion net loss against $16.3 billion in revenue as the company seeks to list Class A shares under the ticker "ANT," with reports putting the target valuation as high as $2 trillion. The filing also flags roughly $417 billion in future compute obligations and warns a clash with the Pentagon could cause "material revenue losses" — a test case, alongside OpenAI's own scramble to raise money before its delayed listing, of whether investors believe the AI buildout's economics.

The draft, reported by Politico, would let the government block release of a model posing an "imminent risk" of killing or injuring more than 50 people or causing over $1 billion in damage — a notable show of regulatory hawkishness from a company now also racing toward a trillion-dollar-plus Nasdaq debut.
