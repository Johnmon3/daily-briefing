US 10-year Treasury yield tops 5% for the first time since 2023 as AI jitters collide with an oil shock

The yield touched 5.014% Monday — its highest since October 2023 — before easing back to about 4.94%, as a stock selloff triggered by Anthropic, OpenAI and xAI leaders' calls to pace AI development collided with inflation fears from Saudi Arabia's shuttered East-West oil pipeline; a break above 5.02% would mark the highest level since the 2008 financial crisis, with real consequences for anyone financing a home, a car or a business two days before the Fed's own rate decision.
