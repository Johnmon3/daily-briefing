Symbiosis halted its Bitcoin Bridge within minutes of catching the September 11 exploit and has already recovered 15 BTC, offering the attacker a 20% white-hat bounty rather than a fight — a reminder that cross-chain bridges, not Bitcoin itself, remain crypto's most exploited seam.

Blockstream refuses to pay ransom for the last $50 million of Bitcoin stolen from its Liquid Network

The self-described "whitehats" who drained nearly 4,000 BTC from Liquid's federation wallet have already returned 85% of it, but Blockstream says it will chase the remaining 598.5 BTC through law enforcement instead of negotiating — a hard line testing whether "ethical hacker" ransom demands survive contact with a company that simply says no.

The September threat report says Moscow-linked cluster GTG-20006 had AI agents handle reconnaissance, exploitation and exfiltration against more than 20 organizations while humans merely supervised, alongside a separate Chinese group targeting roughly 50 organizations — Anthropic's clearest evidence yet that state-backed hacking has shifted from AI-assisted to AI-executed.
