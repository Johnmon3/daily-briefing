Lula completes a historic comeback, winning Brazil's runoff and a fourth term as president

Brazil's electoral court credited Lula with 51.78% of the vote to Flávio Bolsonaro's 48.22% as the count neared completion, making him the first candidate in the country's democratic history to lose the first round and still win the runoff — a result that keeps Latin America's largest economy under leftist control and deals a setback to the Trump-aligned politics that carried Bolsonaro's son to a first-round lead three weeks ago.
