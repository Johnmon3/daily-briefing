Top story

Appeals court rejects the DOJ's bid to block 20,000 California ballots for a second time, setting up a Supreme Court showdown on Election Day eve

A divided 9th Circuit panel ruled Friday that there is "no feasible way" to segregate the disputed ballots without risking "potentially catastrophic effects" on the vote count — the third straight court loss for the administration's push to exclude voters it calls noncitizens. The DOJ is expected to appeal immediately to the Supreme Court, where Justice Elena Kagan oversees emergency appeals from the 9th Circuit, as more than 53 million Americans have already voted ahead of Tuesday's elections for the full House, 35 Senate seats and 39 governorships.
